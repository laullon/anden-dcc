Motor shield bench test

No trains or loads connected. The DC buttons check each polarity; DCC · 3 s sends the roster’s DCC waveform through bridge A. Every test switches off automatically after 3 seconds. Bridge B stays off.

For DC, measure A+ / A− with a voltmeter. For DCC, use your scope setup to inspect the 58 / 100 µs target half-bits. D12 is routed in firmware; no jumper is needed.
